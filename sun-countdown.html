Time Until the Sun Becomes a Red Giant

The Sun will not collapse into a neutron star or explode as a supernova. It is expected to expand into a red giant in about 5 billion years, then eventually become a white dwarf.

What Actually Happens?

Neutron stars and supernovae come from much more massive stars. The Sun's quieter ending is still immense: it will swell, shed its outer layers into space, and leave behind a dense white dwarf core.
